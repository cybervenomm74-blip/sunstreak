    Sunstreak has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your moods, habits and notes are saved only in this browser on your device and are never sent to a server. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads. Sunstreak is not a medical device or medical advice.
